Start Here: Finance Beginner Roadmap

  1. Create a one-page budget and track spending for 30 days.
  2. Build a starter emergency fund of $1,000.
  3. Choose debt payoff strategy (avalanche or snowball).
  4. Open retirement and brokerage accounts for long-term investing.
  5. Automate savings, investing, and bill pay.
